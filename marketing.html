How to play

1. Select a top bottle

Choose a bottle containing the color you want to move. The top color is your next candidate to pour.

2. Pour carefully

Move to a compatible bottle and pour only where colors match, or into an empty slot to keep the flow smooth.

3. Clear the board

When identical colors stack neatly in one column, they lock and disappear, opening a path to the next move.
