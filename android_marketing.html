Short Description

Full Description

MochiDrop is a relaxing logic puzzle game built around colorful pours, satisfying matches, and compact handcrafted levels.

Plan each move, organize every color, and clear the board as the challenge grows. Simple controls make it easy to start, while later puzzles reward careful strategy and calm focus.

Features:

  • 100+ handcrafted puzzle levels
  • Simple tap-based controls
  • Bright, readable visuals and smooth movement
  • Offline-friendly single-player gameplay
  • No account required
  • Optional rewarded-ad opportunities

Advertising and Privacy Choices

Interstitial ads may appear at remotely configured progression checkpoints. Rewarded ads appear only after a player chooses a reward opportunity. Banner ads are currently disabled.

Where required, AppLovin MAX presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy message before Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer attribution and event measurement, or queued advertising are enabled. Players can reopen the available form from Home > Settings > Privacy Choices.
